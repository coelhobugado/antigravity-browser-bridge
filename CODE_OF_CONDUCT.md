# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Antigravity Browser Bridge respectful, welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context patiently.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, browser data or private communications.
- Disclose security concerns privately.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and security boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately weakening authorization or safety controls.
- Using project spaces to promote abuse, spam, credential theft or unauthorized access.
- Repeated disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.
